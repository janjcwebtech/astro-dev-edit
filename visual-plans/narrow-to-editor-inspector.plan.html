One panel

Breadcrumbs on the hover pillThe element tree stays and becomes component-aware, rather than being replaced.

07

Verification

  • Both gates green after every phase — baseline 38 files / 851 tests, tsc --noEmit clean.
  • Element annotations byte-identical with composition on and off — the single most important regression guard in the feature.
  • A chain's last link resolves to the element's own file; a {...Astro.props} forwarder yields inferred or candidates, never a wrong chain.
  • Slotted content is marked as slotted, and its chain is a strict prefix of its DOM parent's.
  • Live data-atx-file count equals the served count on Astro 5.18 and 7.1.1, dev toolbar on and off.
  • No /__dev-edit route answers a CMS path; /health reports no entryEditor or unsplash.
  • A committed fixture site exercises a genuine 3-deep chain — the current playground has only Base/Nav/Footer and cannot.
