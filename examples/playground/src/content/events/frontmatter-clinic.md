A schema starts as six clean fields and ends as a negotiation between four teams. This is a room full of people who have been through that, comparing notes.

## Format

No slides. Someone puts a collection on the screen, describes what it was supposed to hold, and the room works out where it drifted. We usually get through five or six.

## Bring

A schema you are not proud of, and a rough sense of what the content was meant to do. Nothing needs to be tidied up first — the mess is the interesting part.
