Bring a laptop and a site you already maintain. We spend the afternoon on one question: when you change a word in the browser, where does it go?

## What we cover

- Which parts of an Astro page are literal text, and which are expressions the editor has to refuse
- Frontmatter as a typed form rather than a wall of YAML
- Reading a diff as the record of what you just did

## Who it suits

Anyone who ships content to a static site and is tired of the round trip through an editor, a terminal and a deploy. No prior Astro experience assumed, though you will get more out of it if you have built one page.
