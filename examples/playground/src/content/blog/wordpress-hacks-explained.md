Most WordPress hacks are not sophisticated. They rely on a short list of
predictable weaknesses, repeated across millions of sites, and automated at
scale. Understanding the pattern is most of the defence.

## Where attacks actually come from

The overwhelming majority of compromises trace back to three things: outdated
plugins, weak or reused login credentials, and hosting that mixes many sites
on one account. None of these are exotic, which is exactly why they work so
well for attackers running automated scans.

## The habits that stop them

Keep plugins current, enforce strong logins with two-factor authentication,
and put a firewall in front of the site. These are unglamorous, but they close
the doors that automated attacks rely on. Security is maintenance, not a
one-time purchase.
