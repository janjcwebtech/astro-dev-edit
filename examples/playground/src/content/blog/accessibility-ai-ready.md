For years accessibility was framed as a compliance checkbox. That framing is
now out of date. The structured, semantic markup that makes a site usable with
a screen reader is the same markup that AI assistants and agents rely on to
understand a page.

## Structure is the shared language

Clear headings, descriptive link text, labelled forms, and meaningful alt text
are not just kindnesses to users with disabilities. They are the signals that
let any automated system parse what a page means rather than guess from layout.

## The commercial advantage

As more traffic arrives through AI assistants, sites that are already
accessible are already legible to those systems. Accessibility has become your
commercial advantage — the investment pays off twice.
